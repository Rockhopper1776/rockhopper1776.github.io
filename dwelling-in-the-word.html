A different kind of reading plan

Read slowly. Notice more.

Many Bible reading plans are built to help you cover more ground. Dwelling in the Word takes a different path. It invites you to remain with one New Testament book or group of chapters for a month, returning each day until the shape of the text becomes familiar and its words begin to settle into memory.

That repeated attention gives themes, arguments, promises, warnings, and comforts time to emerge with greater clarity. Instead of racing toward a finish line, you are given room to reflect, pray, and carry Scripture into daily life.

Everything you need for a thoughtful monthly rhythm

Each guide brings the reading into focus without crowding out the biblical text. Month by month, you will find:

  • A clear daily reading assignment
  • A doctrinal focus for the month
  • A suggested memory verse and companion resources
  • A prayer focus and study questions for reflection or discussion

Written from a Reformed Christian perspective, the plan works well for personal devotion, family reading, or a small group seeking a more attentive way to read together.
